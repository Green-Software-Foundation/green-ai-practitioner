# Green AI Practitioner: Course Outline [DRAFT]

> ⚠️ **Approval required:** Approve this course outline as the stable, public-facing description of the Green AI Practitioner course.

> ℹ️ **Purpose:** This outline is the shared reference point for all course content decisions. It should be stable once approved — if it needs to change, that signals the course scope or purpose has changed. Approve this before module work begins.

## Tagline

"AI has an environmental cost. This course puts the levers in your hands."

## Course Type

Practitioner (Implementation) — grounded in GSF standards, designed for the practical decisions practitioners make every day.

## Target Audience

Technical practitioners in AI consumer organisations who make decisions about how AI is built and integrated. The course starts with Application Developers and expands to Infrastructure & Operations Engineers, ML/AI Engineers, and Architects/System Designers and other personas.

## Prerequisites

The Foundation module (Introduction to Green AI) is a mandatory prerequisite for all persona modules.

Additional prerequisites by persona module:

- **Application Developer** — comfort with APIs and basic software architecture
- **Infrastructure & Operations** — infrastructure and deployment experience
- **ML/AI Engineer** — ML background
- **Architect/System Designer** — software architecture experience

## Overarching Learning Outcomes

On completing the Foundation module and a persona module, learners will be able to:

1. Explain what Green AI means — and what it does not mean — using the GSF definition
2. Describe the three-pillar environmental measurement framework (carbon, energy, water) and why it matters for software practitioners
3. Make concrete, evidence-based decisions in their role that measurably reduce the environmental impact of AI
4. Identify which Green AI Patterns apply to their decisions and how to implement them

## Course Structure

### Layer 1: Foundation module (mandatory for all learners)

- Introduction to Green AI
- Prerequisite for all persona modules

### Layer 2: Persona modules (self-selected by role after completing the Foundation)

- Application Developer module
- Infrastructure & Operations module
- ML/AI Engineer module
- Architect/System Designer module

Each persona module contains carbon submodules grounded in SCI for AI (ratified December 2025). Energy (SEE for AI) and water (SWE for AI) submodules will be added to each persona module as AI domain specs are ratified.

## Content Hierarchy

### Course → Module → Submodule → Knowledge Unit

A submodule is a single, completable video unit (3–5 minutes) with one learning objective and one quiz assessment. A module is the sum of its submodules and has no fixed length ceiling. Knowledge Units are the structured content artifacts that underpin each submodule — not visible to learners, but the source from which video scripts and AI rulesets are derived.

## Delivery Format

Bite-sized video submodules (3–5 minutes each) with quiz assessment. A structured AI ruleset is published alongside each submodule for use in AI-assisted development contexts.

## Grounding

All content grounded in ratified GSF specifications. SCI for AI (carbon) is ratified — December 2025. SEE for AI (energy) and SWE for AI (water) are forthcoming. No content is published without spec-grounded source authority.

## Success Measure

A learner who completes the Foundation module and their persona module can make a specific decision differently on Monday morning — and knows why it reduces the environmental impact of the AI systems they build.


## v1 MVP Scope

Foundation module + Application Developer module, with a small number of submodules — one or more, to be agreed by the committee. Subsequent submodules and modules published iteratively as Knowledge Units are authored and approved.
